Know Your Legal Rights - Free Aid Eligible

Under Constitution Article 39A & LSA Act 1987 - Every citizen can get free lawyer

⚖️ Right to Free Legal Aid

Under LSA Act Sec 12, if your income < ₹3 Lakhs/year, SC/ST, Woman, Child, Victim of trafficking, Disaster victim - You get FREE advocate, court fees, documentation.

LSA Act 1987 Sec 12

👩 Right to Protection - Women

Domestic Violence Act 2005 gives Right to Residence, Protection Order, Monetary Relief. Call 181, 1091. Free lady advocate from DLSA within 24hrs.

DV Act 2005

🛡️ Consumer Rights

Consumer Protection Act 2019: Right to Refund, Replacement, Compensation up to ₹10L. File at 1915 / consumerhelpline.gov.in. DCDRC must solve in 45 days.

CPA 2019 Sec 34

💻 Cyber Safety Rights

IT Act 2000 Sec 66: Right to report hacking, fraud within 2 hours (Golden Hour) at 1930 & cybercrime.gov.in. Bank must freeze amount.

IT Act Sec 66

🏠 Property Rights

Karnataka Land Revenue Act: Right to RTC, EC, Mutation. Check Kaveri portal. 12 years possession = ownership right. Free DLSA lawyer for land dispute.

KLR Act 1964 Sec 128

👷 Labour Rights

Minimum Wages Act 1948, Payment of Wages Act 1936: Right to Salary on 7th, PF, ESI, Overtime. Salary < ₹25k = free labour lawyer. Call 1800-425-2667.

MW Act 1948
✅ Are You Eligible for FREE Lawyer? Income < ₹3 Lakhs/year = 100% Free - NALSA 15100

Women, SC/ST, Children, Senior Citizens, Disaster victims, Industrial workers are automatically eligible - No income proof needed.
