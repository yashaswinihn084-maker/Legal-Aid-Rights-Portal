Know Your Legal Rights

Right to Equality

Every person is entitled to equality before the law and equal protection of the laws.

Right to Freedom

Citizens have constitutional freedoms subject to reasonable restrictions provided by law.

Right Against Exploitation

The Constitution provides protections against trafficking and certain forms of forced labour.

Right to Constitutional Remedies

Constitutional remedies allow individuals to approach appropriate courts for enforcement of fundamental rights.

Right to Legal Aid

Eligible persons can receive legal services through legal services authorities according to applicable law and eligibility rules.

Consumer Rights

Consumers have legal protections relating to goods and services under applicable consumer protection laws.
