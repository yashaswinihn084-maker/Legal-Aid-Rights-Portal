About Legal Aid & Rights Awareness Portal

The Legal Aid & Rights Awareness Portal is a web-based platform designed to provide people with simple and accessible information about their legal rights and laws.

The main purpose of this portal is to improve legal awareness among citizens and help them understand basic legal information without difficulty.

The portal provides information about fundamental rights, important laws, legal procedures and available legal assistance.

This project is developed as an educational web application using Python, Flask, HTML, CSS and a database.
