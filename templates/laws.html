Important Laws

Constitution of India

The Constitution of India is the supreme legal framework of the country. It establishes the structure of government and provides fundamental rights and duties.

Consumer Protection Act, 2019

This law provides a framework for protecting consumer interests and addressing consumer complaints and disputes.

Right to Information Act, 2005

The Right to Information framework allows eligible citizens to seek information from public authorities, subject to the provisions and exemptions of the law.

Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987

This law provides the legal framework for legal services authorities and the provision of free legal services to eligible persons.

Information Technology Act, 2000

The Information Technology Act provides a legal framework relating to electronic records, electronic transactions and specified offences involving computer resources.
