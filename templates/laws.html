Important Laws for Citizens - With Sections

Instant solution provided after you submit request - These laws will be cited in your case

📜

Legal Services Authority Act, 1987 - Sec 12

Free legal aid for income < 3 Lakhs, SC/ST, Women, Children. Free advocate, court fee, paper book. NALSA Toll-Free 15100, KSLSA 080-22111718.

Section: Sec 12(a) to (h) - Criteria for free legal services | Punishment for violation: Contempt of Court
🛒

Consumer Protection Act, 2019

Product defect, service deficiency, fraud. File at 1915, consumerhelpline.gov.in. Get refund + compensation up to ₹10 Lakhs. DCDRC must decide in 45 days.

Section: Sec 2(7) Consumer, Sec 2(11) Deficiency, Sec 34(1) Jurisdiction | Helpline 1915
💻

Information Technology Act, 2000

Online fraud, hacking, OTP scam. GOLDEN HOUR - Report within 2 hrs at 1930 & cybercrime.gov.in. Bank must freeze transaction. Don't delete proof.

Section: Sec 43 Penalty for damage, Sec 66 Hacking (3 years), Sec 66C Identity theft, Sec 66D Cheating
👩‍⚖️

Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005

Physical, mental, economic violence. Right to Residence (Sec 17), Protection Order (Sec 18), Monetary Relief. Call Women Helpline 181, 1091.

Section: Sec 12 Application to Magistrate, Sec 17 Right to residence, Sec 18 Protection order
🏞️

Karnataka Land Revenue Act, 1964 & Registration Act

RTC, EC, Mutation, Kaveri portal. 12 years adverse possession. File injunction under CPC Order 39. Free DLSA lawyer for property dispute.

Section: KLR Sec 128 Record of Rights, Sec 129 Mutation | CPC Order 39 Injunction
👷

Labour Laws - Payment of Wages Act 1936 + MW Act 1948

Salary must be paid by 7th. PF, ESI mandatory. Overtime double. If salary < ₹25000, free labour advocate from DLSA. File at Shram Suvidha portal.

Section: PW Act Sec 15 Claim, MW Act Sec 12 Payment of minimum wages | Helpline 1800-425-2667
